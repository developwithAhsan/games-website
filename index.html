About Vice City Web Edition

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Web Edition brings the legendary open world experience of 1986 Miami directly into your browser. Set in a sprawling, neon-soaked metropolis inspired by 1980s Miami, Vice City stands as one of the most iconic environments in gaming history.

The story follows Tommy Vercetti, a mobster recently released from prison after serving fifteen years. He is sent to Vice City by his former boss Sonny Forelli to oversee a drug deal. When the deal is ambushed and both the money and the drugs are lost, Tommy is left to pick up the pieces.

As he works to recover what was lost and take revenge on the Forelli family, Tommy becomes involved with the city's various criminal organizations and power players. Over time, he builds his own network of businesses and influence, ultimately rising to control Vice City's criminal underworld.

This technical port is built on the reVCDOS codebase, which utilizes the reverse-engineered reVC engine. This version requires no downloads and no installation because assets stream dynamically as you play. It is a complete, self-contained experience that preserves every mission from the original 2002 release.

Key Features:

  • Integrated Save Manager: You can upload your existing .b save files from a PC or PS2 to resume your progress. You can also download backups to keep your virtual progress safe.
  • Instant Access: There are no large initial downloads. The WebAssembly engine loads in seconds and streams the map as you drive.
  • Cross-Platform Support: Play with a standard keyboard and mouse on desktop or use integrated touch controls on mobile devices.

Meet Muhammad Ahsan — My Story

Hello! My name is Muhammad Ahsan, a student and passionate developer living in Pakistan 🇵🇰. I am self-driven, deeply curious about technology, and currently on an exciting journey learning modern software development and web architecture.

My love for computer programming began with a simple question: "How can we make complex 3D video games accessible to anyone in the world without requiring expensive hardware or installation files?" As a big fan of classic open-world games like Grand Theft Auto: Vice City and GTA III, I wanted to combine retro gaming nostalgia with the modern magic of browser technologies.

That spark inspired the creation of GTABrowser — a dedicated web application designed to showcase high-performance C++ WebAssembly (WASM) reverse-engineered engine ports directly in the browser.

🚀 Our Mission

To democratize access to classic 3D games through modern web technology, preserving digital gaming heritage and making open-world experiences playable anywhere with zero friction.

⚡ How It Works

Using reVC / re3 C++ engines compiled to WebAssembly, WebGL 2.0 rendering, and browser OPFS local file storage, GTABrowser runs full 3D game engines natively at 60 FPS inside standard web browsers.

🛠️ Technology & Philosophy

  • Zero Installation: Play instantly in Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Microsoft Edge.
  • Private Local Save States: Your game progress is saved safely inside your device's local Origin Private File System (OPFS).
  • Responsive Experience: Mobile touch virtual gamepad emulation and desktop keyboard/gamepad controller support.
  • Community First: Free, open, student-led project built with genuine care for web performance and user experience.
